Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

AI Boot Room (aibootroom.com, the YouTube channel @AiBootRoom and the X account @AiBootRoom) publishes football market analysis. The project is run from Spain, so the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Spanish data protection law (LOPDGDD 3/2018) and the Spanish law on information society services (LSSI 34/2002) apply.

Short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no forms, no newsletter. We never ask for your name or e-mail.
  • Google Analytics runs only if you press “Accept”. “Reject” is just as easy and changes nothing on the site.
  • Videos come from YouTube and start only when you press play.
  • We do not sell data, we do not show ads and we do not work with bookmakers.

Who is responsible

The data controller is the person who runs AI Boot Room, based in Spain. Contact: send a direct message to @AiBootRoom on X. We answer privacy requests within one month.

International transfers

GitHub and Google are based in the United States and are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognises as adequate protection.
