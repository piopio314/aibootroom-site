Five wins against seven unbeaten European nights

Brighton come to Czechia with five wins in a row, 17 goals scored and two conceded. Jablonec have lost their last two matches, including a 2-3 home defeat by Plzen on Sunday. The results point one way.

But the home side has an argument. Jablonec are unbeaten in seven European home matches, according to Yahoo Sports [7], and their coach Luboš Kozel has called the visit of Brighton a 'beautiful' game [8].

The league phase table adds weight to the away case. Brighton sit first after one match, a 4-0 win over Kauno Žalgiris, while Jablonec are 23rd with no points after losing 2-3 at Freiburg. Both teams played on Sunday, so both have had four days to recover.

What the xG says about the two 2-3 defeats

Jablonec's last two matches both finished 2-3, and the expected goals explain the difference between them. At home to Plzen they created 3.16 and allowed 1.11, which is more than enough to win, and they lost. In Freiburg they created 1.18 and allowed 1.87. Across their last five they average 1.69 xG created and 0.98 allowed, yet they have conceded seven goals in that time.

Brighton's run is built on chance quality, not only on finishing. The 4-0 win over Kauno Žalgiris came with 4.02 xG created and 0.04 allowed, and the 5-0 over Crystal Palace on Sunday with 2.0 against 0.77. One honest note: the 3-0 win over Arsenal in September came with 1.31 xG to Arsenal's 1.63 — they won that one without controlling the chances.
