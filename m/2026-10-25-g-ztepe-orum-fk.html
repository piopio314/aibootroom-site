Göztepe's home drought meets Çorum's away storm

A home crowd, a home problem

Göztepe return to the Gürsel Aksel Stadium on Sunday [3]. They come back to a crowd that has not seen a home league win this season: three home matches, three without a win [2]. The club sits 13th with nine points from eight games.

Çorum FK arrive in a very different mood. Burak Yılmaz's side are sixth on 13 points and have won four of their last five league matches, including a 3-0 at Gaziantep FK and a 2-1 at home to Rizespor [7][8]. Turkish outlet Fotomaç calls them the form team of the Süper Lig, describing a "Çorum FK storm" with Cengiz Ünder leading it [11].

That contrast is the whole match. The team with the home ground is below the team without it, and the team in form is the visitor. Even so, the market makes Göztepe the favourite at 43.7% against Çorum's 30.1%. Favourites, but not by much.

Göztepe create, Çorum convert

Göztepe's last two matches were both away and both wins: 4-0 at Eyüpspor and 2-1 at Kocaelispor [1]. The expected goals tell two different stories. At Eyüpspor they created 3.15 and allowed 1.33, and the scoreline matched the chances. At Kocaelispor they created just 0.83 and allowed 1.61, so they won a game in which the opponent made the better chances. The home pattern is similar: the 2-2 with Rizespor came with 2.78 expected goals against 0.78, and the 2-4 defeat to Gaziantep FK came with 3.26 created. Göztepe make chances; they have not turned them into home points. Across five games they average 2.36 expected goals for and 1.46 against.

Çorum's numbers run the other way: 14 goals in five matches from 1.76 expected goals a game. They beat Rizespor 2-1 with 0.71 expected goals and won 5-1 at Samsunspor with 1.73. They are scoring more than their chances suggest, and the week has been kind to them — nine days of rest to Göztepe's seven, plus three days off after the Rizespor win, as reported by Sondakika [10].

Returns, not absences

Göztepe's team news is about who is back rather than who is out. According to Egetelgraf, Sonko Sundberg and Novatus Miroshi have returned to the squad, and Miroshi scored in the 2-1 win at Kocaelispor [4]. The same outlet reports that coach Stanimir Stoilov has seen his expectations met after those returns and the two wins [5]. No senior player is listed as unavailable for the home side.

Çorum also come with no listed absences, after a week built on rest and a return to training under Yılmaz [8][10]. For a team that has scored 14 goals in five matches, an unchanged attacking group is the part that matters most.

A quarter of a goal between them

The football and the price agree on one thing

Put the two halves together. Göztepe are back at home, creating 2.36 expected goals a game, and still waiting for a home league win. Çorum are sixth, sharper in front of goal, and arrive on three straight wins. The market weighs the home ground and the chance creation a little heavier than the table and the results, which is why the home side are favourites, why the handicap is only a quarter of a goal, and why nearly three goals are expected. Göztepe are the most likely single winner on Sunday, but with 43.7% they are short of a coin flip, and the draw and Çorum together take more than half the match — a first home win of the season is far from certain.
