A debut night against a team in form

Inter Club d'Escaldes play their first home match in the main phase of a European competition on Thursday, and the visitors are a side in the middle of a Romanian title chase. Craiova have won four of their last five and scored ten goals along the way, while the Andorrans sit 33rd of 36 in the Conference League table after a 0-2 defeat at Mjällby [7].

The opening round shapes the early picture. Craiova drew 1-1 at home with Getafe and are still looking for their first win of the league phase [6] [3]. Inter have already seen how quickly the level rises after losing their opening match.

Filipe Coelho has spent the week making sure nobody in his squad treats this as a light evening. "We want to win, nothing more," he said in Andorra, adding that his players must take the match very seriously [1].

One possible recall, one familiar face

Steven Nsimba could come back into Craiova's starting eleven, according to sport.ro, with Coelho saying he has already settled on his team [4]. The Romanians have rotated through a busy run of league and European games, so a fresh attacking option matters for a night when chances may be few.

On the other side there is a familiar face: Juan Camara, once on loan at Craiova, is now a regular for Inter and will meet his old club, according to digisport.ro [8].

Favourites, but by about a goal

Across the ten bookmakers we compare, with the margin removed, Craiova are 59.6% to win, the draw 22.7% and Inter 17.7% — more than three times the Andorrans' chance. That is consistent with a side on a fifth straight Superliga win meeting a club playing its first main-phase campaign.

The handicap tells a more careful story. Inter are given a one-goal start, and even with that help the market rates Craiova about nine-tenths of a goal better, at a fair line of 0.91. A single-goal away win would leave that line close to level, which is what a controlled Craiova performance looks like.

The goals line sits at 2.53, with over 2.5 at 50.6% and both teams scoring at 49.5% — the market is split on whether this game reaches three goals. Craiova's recent matches have been tight: ten goals in five games, but one conceded, and their Getafe draw finished 1-1.
