Seventeenth against twentieth, in October

Genoa and Venezia are the bottom two in Serie A. Seven games in, the gap between them is a single point: Genoa 17th on four, Venezia 20th on three. Sunday at Marassi is a relegation fight before the end of October — De Rossi's words, according to Gazzetta — with both clubs already in the drop zone [1].

The mood around the two teams could hardly be more different. Venezia beat Napoli 1-0 in Davide Nicola's first match in charge [7], their first Serie A points of the season, with Akor Adams scoring [8]. Genoa lost 0-2 at Roma the same weekend and have one league win all season.

Daniele De Rossi called Sunday a relegation battle but "not a last chance" [1], and Italian media are describing the fixture as a match salvezza, a fight for survival [2]. The table says the same in numbers: between them the two sides have two league wins from fourteen games.

Genoa create little, Venezia concede a lot

Genoa's last five league matches produced 1.13 expected goals a game going forward and 1.15 against — a team in low-scoring, tight matches. The one win came at home to Fiorentina, 2-1 with 1.46 xG. In Rome they managed 0.47 xG, which is why the 0-2 was not a surprise on the chance count.

Venezia's games are the opposite: 2.27 xG against per game across their last five. They created 2.48 xG at Atalanta and lost 1-3. Against Napoli they won 1-0 with 0.98 xG against 2.36 — Kevin De Bruyne had a penalty saved [9], and the Guardian called it a "Houdini act in Venice" [10]. Both clubs have had eight days since their last match, so freshness is not a factor for either.

A quarter of a goal, and a lower goals line

Across the ten bookmakers we compare, Genoa are 45.0% to win, the draw 30.1% and Venezia 24.9%. That makes the home side clear favourites, but the draw takes a large share and Venezia's chance is not small. The handicap line is a quarter of a goal in Genoa's favour, and the fair line sits at -0.35, having come in from -0.4 in recent days. In plain terms: the market rates Genoa about a quarter of a goal better, and a draw would not be enough to make them winners on that measure.

The goals line is 2.5, with Over at 48.1% and Both teams to score at 54.2% — a lean to fewer goals that fits Genoa's low chance creation. Recent history backs the home edge too: both of the last two Serie A meetings finished 2-0 to the home side.

The ground, not the form

Genoa are favourites because this game is at Marassi and because Venezia have lost six of seven league matches. It is not because Genoa are playing well: one win, one draw and five defeats, and 1.13 xG per game is the profile of a team that struggles to build chances. Venezia's problem is the other end — 2.27 xG against per game says their matches are open, and open matches away from home are hard to control. That is why the home advantage is real but thin, a quarter of a goal on the handicap and 45% in the result market, and why the goals price has moved down rather than up. A home win is more likely than not, but only just.
