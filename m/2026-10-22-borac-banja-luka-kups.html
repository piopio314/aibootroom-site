Two teams on zero, one simple question

Both sides arrive with nothing. Borac Banja Luka sit 26th of the 36 teams in the Conference League league phase and KuPS are 31st, separated only by goal difference after opening defeats — Borac 2-1 at Panathinaikos, KuPS 4-2 at home to Trabzonspor [7]. So Thursday in Bosnia is the simplest kind of fixture: the winner banks its first points, and the loser starts looking at the table in a less comfortable way.

The home side's case is the stronger one. Borac have won four of their last five, all in the domestic league, and they come in off a free weekend. KuPS have lost three of five, and they played on Sunday.

The doubt sits in Europe. The one time Borac stepped up in class this month, in Athens, they managed 0.23 expected goals against 3.8 for the hosts. A team that creates almost nothing at this level can still be the home favourite against a weaker opponent — but it explains why the market rates them clearly, not heavily.

Four wins at home, one hard lesson abroad

Borac's run reads LWWWW: four straight wins, nine goals scored and five conceded across the five matches. Those wins came against BSK Banja Luka and Zrinjski at home and Siroki Brijeg and Sloga Doboj away, with scorelines of 2-0, 1-0, 3-2 and 2-1 — margins of two, one, one and one. That is a solid base, and it is the football underneath the home favourite tag.

KuPS's five read WLLLW, with an average of 1.91 expected goals against. The clearest example is the 4-2 home defeat to Trabzonspor, when they allowed 3.61 xG [5]. There was a 2-0 win over VPS on Sunday with 1.69 xG for and 0.8 against, so they can defend when the level drops. Rest also splits them here: Borac have had seven days, KuPS four.

Time, not absences, is the team news

The difference between these two squads this week is the calendar. Borac had no Premier League of Bosnia and Herzegovina fixture last weekend, reported by Press.rs, so they have had a full week to prepare [4]. KuPS have played Thursday and Sunday in each of the last two weeks, which is a heavy load for a side that also has to travel.

Borac's coach Savić said his team will reach a result through quality play, according to Bosnian media [3]. The mood at home is clear: Bosnian media report that Borac are chasing their first points of the league phase in front of their own fans [1]. From Finland, pallomeri.net calls Borac the favourites while rating them considerably weaker than Trabzonspor, KuPS's first-round opponents [6] — a step down in class, but not enough to make KuPS favourites.

A clear favourite, a low-scoring night

Our read: the fresher side, not a certain one

Borac have the better run, the fresher legs and the home crowd, and the market makes them clear favourites at more than double KuPS's chance. Even so, the European sample is thin and unkind — 0.23 expected goals in Athens — while KuPS have already shown they can be cut open by good teams and can hold their shape against weaker ones. A fair goal total just under 2.5 says the market sees a controlled game rather than an open one. Borac's first point of the league phase is more likely than not, and it is the fresher legs that make the difference.
