A favourite near the bottom

Friday night's match opens the ninth round of the Primeira Liga [5]. Vitória SC go into it 15th of 18 with five points from eight games, yet the market still makes them favourites against Marítimo — 59.5% to win, against the visitors' 16.4%.

The table argues the other way. Marítimo are 11th on eight points, three clear of their hosts and two places higher. So the home favouritism is a call on this fixture, not on the season: home advantage, the matchup, and a history that has been one-sided for years.

Vitória have won the last four meetings between the clubs, each by a single goal — 2-1, 1-0, 0-1 and 1-2 — with a 0-0 before that. Marítimo, meanwhile, average 0.48 expected goals a game this season. The shape of the evening is easy to guess: one team with the ball and the chances, one team with very little.

Vitória create, Marítimo don't

Vitória's league run reads LDLDL, including a 0-2 defeat at Benfica before the international break in which they managed 0.43 xG against 2.76. Their home games tell a different story. They drew 0-0 with Casa Pia after 2.48 xG and allowed only 0.21, then drew 1-1 with Moreirense with 1.07 xG against 0.52.

The problem is finishing, not creating: an xG average of 1.52 in recent matches, but only three goals in their last five in all competitions. Marítimo's numbers go the other way. They had 0.12 xG at home to Benfica and 0.19 at home to Porto, and two of their five goals in the last five matches came in the 2-1 cup win at Alcochetense. Both sides had a cup game last week — Vitória six days ago, Marítimo five — so neither arrives short of rest.

Two out for six weeks, one doubt

Vitória confirmed that goalkeeper Juan Castillo and defender Francisco Dias have sprains and will be out for up to six weeks [1]. Losing a goalkeeper and a defender together matters for a side whose home defensive numbers have been good: 0.21 xG allowed to Casa Pia, 0.52 to Moreirense.

There is also a doubt. According to Notícias ao Minuto, a Vitória player with eight appearances this season is uncertain for Friday's match [3]. That is reported, not confirmed, so the club has not ruled him out. Even so, it thins a home squad that is already near the bottom of the table.

About 0.9 goals better, not a full goal

The win chances, with the bookmakers' margin removed across the 10 firms we compare, are Vitória 59.5%, draw 24.1%, Marítimo 16.4%. That is more than three times the visitors' chance. The main handicap line is -0.75, which in plain terms means Vitória are rated around three-quarters of a goal better than Marítimo — the sort of gap that usually produces a one-goal home win rather than a comfortable one.

The fairest line has moved from a full goal to -0.87 in recent days. The gap is still clear, but smaller than it was. The goals line sits at 2.5, with over priced at 48.2%, so the market leans to under; both teams to score is at 47.8%. The fair goals line slipped from 2.55 to 2.43, which fits a game with one team creating and the other not.

What moved this week points the same way. The home handicap price fell 3.4 points, the over 2.5 price fell 3.1, and Vitória's win chance came down 2.8 points. The club confirmed the two absences a day before the biggest of those moves — consistent with the news being priced in. Vitória remain favourites, rated a little less strongly than before.

Direction agreed, size trimmed

The football and the market agree on the direction and differ on the size. Vitória are the better side here, at home, against an opponent that has created almost nothing all season and has won none of the last five meetings between the clubs. But they are also a 15th-placed team that has failed to score in two of its last five matches, and they now lose a goalkeeper and a defender for six weeks.

That is why the line has come down from a full goal to 0.87 rather than disappeared. The one-goal history between these clubs, the 48.2% on over 2.5 and Marítimo's 0.48 xG all point to the same picture: a tight, low-scoring game where the home side has the chances. Vitória at 59.5% are favourites, not certainties — and the trimmed line says exactly that.
