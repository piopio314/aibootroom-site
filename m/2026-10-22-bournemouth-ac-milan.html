A good week meets a wounded giant

Bournemouth have played two Europa League games and won both. On Thursday they host AC Milan at the Vitality, and the bookmakers we compare make the home side favourites at 47.7%, with the draw at 25.7% and Milan at 26.6%.

The tie changed shape in the final hours. Milan lost Goncalo Ramos and Samuel Chukwueze to injury, both reported by World Soccer Talk citing Sky Italia [7][8]. Milan's win chance has fallen about 2.5 points to 26.6%, while Bournemouth's has risen to 47.7% — consistent with the market repricing an attack now short of two options.

The picture is simple. Bournemouth are 5th of the 36 Europa League teams with six points from two games; Milan are 16th with three, having already lost at home to Benfica. Marco Rose called the tie a "huge game" against a club he described as six-time Champions League winners [1], and his team arrive after their first league win of the season [4].

What the xG says about Bournemouth's run

Bournemouth's 2-0 win over Sunderland was their first league win of the season, according to livescore.com [4]. According to sports.yahoo.com, the numbers behind it were heavy: 29 shots and an xG of 4.32 [5]. Earlier in the run were a home win over Sturm Graz in Europe and a win away at Real Sociedad, while their two defeats came against Liverpool and at Chelsea; the Stamford Bridge scoreline looks worse than the performance.

Milan's run is mixed in a different way. They scored four away at Red Bull Salzburg with 3.45 xG, and beat Atalanta 3-2 on Sunday with 2.58. But the 0-2 home loss to Benfica in this competition and a 1-1 draw at Sassuolo show a side that can go quiet. Both teams had four days between matches, so neither side has a rest advantage.

Two Milan attackers out, one Bournemouth midfielder missing

Ramos has calf discomfort from training and is out, with further tests expected [7]. Chukwueze has a problem in his left hip and also misses the trip [8]. That is two forward options gone on the eve of the match, on top of Saelemaekers (ankle, 218 minutes this season) and Terracciano (suspended). OneFootball reports that Ruben Amorim is preparing significant rotation, with Loftus-Cheek, Cisse and Camarda involved [9]. Amorim is also unbeaten in his first seven Serie A games at the club, per Yahoo Sports [10].

Bournemouth's loss is Antoine Scott: 536 minutes this season, a goal, an assist and a rating of 7.3 — a starter, not a squad player. Sportsmole reports a two-week absence covering this game and the three that follow [3], and the Bournemouth Echo reports that Junior Kroupi has returned to training but will not play here [2]. Petrovic remains the starting goalkeeper, according to Gazzetta, with Di Gregorio limited to one Carabao Cup appearance since his loan move [6].

Our read: a home side trusted, a giant short of weapons

Bournemouth are favourites because they create a lot at home — 4.4 xG on Sunday, 2.88 against Sturm Graz — and because Milan arrive without Ramos and Chukwueze. But 47.7% is not dominance, and Milan's 4-0 at Salzburg is a reminder that they travel well in this competition. The market has joined the two stories: a modest move toward the home side, a fair line drifting out to -0.43, and a goals line of 2.75 that expects chances at both ends. Bournemouth are the side more likely to win on Thursday; Milan can still hurt them.
