A first point is already needed

Plzen are 31st in the 36-team Europa League table with no points after two matches. They lost at home to Union St. Gilloise and away to Ferencváros, and a first win here would revive their hopes of reaching the knockout rounds [3]. Another defeat would leave them with a very long road.

Levski Sofia arrive in a very different mood. They have one point from two European games — a 1-1 draw at NEC Nijmegen and a 0-1 home loss to Red Bull Salzburg — and they have conceded only twice in their last five matches in all competitions. Their recent defensive solidity is the theme of pre-match coverage, according to DailySports [7]. That defensive record is the main obstacle for a Plzen side that expects to have the ball.

There is also a personal thread. Three current Plzen players arrived from Levski in the recent past, according to Plzeňský deník [5]. It adds a little edge to a game that already matters a lot for the home team.

Sojka is the home question

The narrow favourite

Plzen need a first point, and the market makes them 53.7% favourites at home. But Levski have conceded just twice in five matches, and Plzen's three games with xG data show them conceding 2.25 expected goals on average. That is why Plzen are clear favourites rather than overwhelming ones, and why the market's expected margin is about one goal. The home side should have the ball; the question is whether they can turn it into enough goals against a team set up to defend. A home win is the more likely outcome at 53.7%, though Levski's defensive record means the probability stops well short of certainty. The draw (24.6%) and a Levski win (21.7%) together are not far off the home chance.
