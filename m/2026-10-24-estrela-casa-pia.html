A homecoming after two bad Saturdays

Estrela have lost their last two league games and still go into Saturday as clear favourites. The market makes them 56.0% to win, roughly three times Casa Pia's 18.8%, and what separates the two sides is the table rather than the last fortnight: Estrela are 10th with ten points, Casa Pia are 17th of 18 with four.

Saturday is also a homecoming. Estrela return to the Estádio José Gomes for the ninth round, and the club confirmed that a newly laid pitch will be unveiled in this match [6][7]. The old surface was renovated during the international break after criticism, according to maisfutebol [8].

Casa Pia arrive from a 1-0 win at Alpendorada in the Taça de Portugal on Sunday, and their league record is the problem: one win in eight, four points, a goal difference of -14. Even so, that single win came away from home, 2-1 at Estoril, so the trip is not automatically a lost cause.

Estrela's results run under their chances

The 0-1 defeat at Arouca on 11 October was not a game Estrela were overrun in. They created 1.45 xG to Arouca's 0.74 and lost to a single goal. The heavier afternoon came at home to Academico Viseu, 0-2, with 0.84 xG against 2.54, the largest chance concession in their recent run. Their 2-1 win over Braga, by contrast, came with 0.57 xG to 0.61: a result built on very little.

Across their last five matches Estrela average 1.18 xG for and 1.28 against, the profile of a mid-table team. Casa Pia average 1.1 for and 1.66 against, and their away days look better than their home ones: 2.17 xG in the win at Estoril, but only 0.21 xG in the 0-0 at Vitória SC, a point taken from almost nothing. Estrela have had seven days' rest since their cup tie, Casa Pia six.

A red card in midfield, a defender back

Casa Pia's cup win cost them Abdu Dafé, sent off at Alpendorada and unavailable here, according to abola.pt [3]. Losing a midfield option matters more than usual in a match where they will spend long spells without the ball. The same outlet reports that defender David Sousa is available again after suspension, so the defence is closer to full strength [2].

Patrick Sequeira started in goal in the cup, his first start of the season, and kept a clean sheet [1]. crhoy.com reports that progress in the competition is good news for his chances of more minutes [4]. For Estrela, record.pt reports that 18-year-old forward Henrique Poniewas is gaining space in the first team after his Taça debut, with Mitongo and Arévalo unavailable [9].

Favourites by a margin, not by a goal

The win chances come from the ten bookmakers we compare, with their margin removed: Estrela 56.0%, the draw 25.2%, Casa Pia 18.8%. The handicap says the same thing in another way. The fair line is -0.75, which rates Estrela about three-quarters of a goal the better team, so the market expects a winning margin and not just a win. Casa Pia take a 0.75-goal head start, and a single-goal Estrela victory does not fully clear that line.

The goals market points to a tighter afternoon than the result market does. The line is 2.5, but Over is only 47.7% and the fair total is 2.41, just under two and a half. Both teams to score sits at 49.6%, below even odds. The week's moves have been small: Estrela's win chance eased 0.8 points and the handicap moved 1.0 point towards Casa Pia, consistent with two straight league defeats, while the home side stays firmly ahead.

Quality over recent results

The through-line is that the market trusts Estrela's quality and the table gap more than the last two scorelines. The homecoming, the new pitch and a Casa Pia side with a -14 goal difference all pull one way, and the handicap asks Estrela to win by more than a single goal. The counterweight is the goal line: a tight, low-scoring game is the more likely shape, and Estrela have already lost this season on a day when they created more than their opponent. At 56.0% they are favourites, not certainties.
