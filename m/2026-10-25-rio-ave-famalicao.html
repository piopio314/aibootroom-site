Level on points, level on problems

Two clubs on seven points, one place apart, both looking down the table. Famalicão are 13th in the Primeira Liga, Rio Ave are 14th, and Sunday's match at the Estádio do Rio Ave FC is the kind that decides which of them spends the winter near the wrong end.

What makes it unusual is the timing. Famalicão announced on Monday that Carlos Carvalhal had been dismissed after almost three months in charge, two days after a 1-0 cup defeat away to Liga 3 side Paredes [6][9]. A Bola reports that assistant Ricardo Silva is expected to take the team on Sunday, with a new head coach only in place for a home game in November [7].

Rio Ave have had the calmer week. They beat Louletano 1-0 in the cup with a goal at the death [1], and Cidade Hoje reports the mood around the club has improved since Vasco Seabra took charge [3]. The winner on Sunday moves clear of a direct rival; the loser stays in the group at the bottom.

Rio Ave ship chances, Famalicão ship goals

Rio Ave's last five read WWLDL, and the details behind it are poor. Over that run they created chances worth 0.88 xG a game and conceded 2.04 — more than twice as much. The two league defeats show the pattern: 0-1 at Alverca with 0.29 xG created, and 0-4 at Santa Clara with 0.32. Their home matches went better, 3-2 against Nacional and 3-3 with Estrela, but both times they gave up real chances.

Famalicão's run is LLWDD, with 1.0 xG created and 1.84 conceded per game. The 4-0 win at Nacional is the best result either side has in this stretch, but the last two matches were defeats — 1-4 at home to Alverca, where they created 0.4 xG and allowed 2.92, and then Paredes in the cup. On rest, the sides are almost identical: eight days for Rio Ave, seven for Famalicão.

The absence that matters is on the bench

Famalicão travel without a permanent head coach. Record reports that Custódio is on the shortlist to replace Carvalhal, and Cidade Hoje reports that Ricardo Silva is running training in the meantime [8][11]. Diadejogo reported three players were unavailable for the Paredes tie [10].

In goal, Record reports that Ennio van der Gouw started the cup match but that Vítor Hugo is expected to reclaim the league starting spot [2]. That is a smaller question than the obvious one: how a squad responds in its first league game after the coach who signed many of them has gone.

The market still trusts the visitors

The win probabilities favour Famalicão at 46.4%, against 25.1% for Rio Ave and 28.5% for the draw. That is a clear edge for a club that announced the dismissal of its coach on Monday [6], and it is consistent with the fixture more than the form: Rio Ave have not beaten Famalicão in any of the last five meetings, three of them draws. Even so, the visitors' win chance has come down by 3.8 points in recent days, to 46.4% — some of the doubt is now reflected in the probability.

The handicap is written with Rio Ave given a half-goal start, which means Famalicão have to win outright to justify favouritism. The fair line has narrowed from 0.51 to 0.40, so the visitors are still rated the better team, but by less than a week ago. The goals line sits at 2.25, with over at 51.8% and both teams to score at 50.4%, and the market's expected total has come down from 2.45 to 2.32 — consistent with two goalless draws in the last two meetings and two attacks that are not creating much.
