Top of the table, and a derby on top of it

Monday night at RAMS Park brings the Intercontinental Derby, one of football's biggest rivalries [4], and it lands with the top of the Süper Lig packed tight: Galatasaray first on 19 points, Fenerbahçe third on 16 from the same eight games. The Turkish Football Federation confirmed the fixture stays on Monday 26 October at 21.30 local time, in week nine of the league [10].

Galatasaray arrive from a 4-2 win away at Lille, coming from behind twice for their first points of this Champions League campaign [1], and Victor Osimhen scored against his former club for his first European goal of the season [3]. Okan Buruk turned his attention to the derby straight after the final whistle, according to legit.ng [2].

Fenerbahçe arrive from the opposite feeling. They let a two-goal lead slip late against Slavia Prague at home, drawing 2-2 [5], and Turkish media compared them unfavourably with Sabah, who beat the same Czech side 2-0 a week earlier, as reported by idman.biz [7]. Add the reported news that Marco Asensio will be out for around three months after knee surgery, per beIN Sports [8], and the away week has been a difficult one.

Galatasaray's results are ahead of their chances

Six wins in eight league games is a strong base, but the leaders' goal difference is only +6, which is thin for a side on 19 points. Across their last five in all competitions they scored nine and conceded eleven, with 1.5 expected goals created per game and 1.84 allowed. They have not been dominant in chance terms: at Lille they made 1.66 expected goals to Lille's 1.92 and still took the match 4-2, while at home to Barcelona they made 0.42 and lost 1-4.

Fenerbahçe's recent numbers point the other way. They average 2.57 expected goals across their last five, more than Galatasaray's 1.5 over the same span, and their 8-0 win over Eyüpspor came with 5.14 expected goals. Their league goal difference of +16 against the leaders' +6 says the same thing. The 0-6 at Aston Villa is the warning inside those numbers: they created 1.75 that night and allowed 3.15. Fenerbahçe also had one more day to recover, six rest days to Galatasaray's five.

Fenerbahçe's week of injuries

The biggest team news is Asensio. beIN Sports reported that he will be out for around three months after knee surgery, having played through the problem to help the team [8]. That is not a one-match absence: Fenerbahçe lose a senior attacking option for the derby and for the rest of the autumn.

The second question is Nathan Ake. Hürriyet reported that he could not continue after picking up an injury against Slavia Prague [6], which leaves his fitness as the main open item on the away team sheet. İsmail Kartal began derby preparations with the players who had featured against Slavia doing regeneration work [9].

This is the league leader at home against the side that creates more than it does. Galatasaray have the table, the ground and a 4-2 win in Europe behind them, and the market makes them favourites.
