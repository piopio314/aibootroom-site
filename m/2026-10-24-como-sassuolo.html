A historic Tuesday, a difficult Saturday

Como's week does not get simpler. On Tuesday they beat Manchester United 4-1 in the Champions League [1], a night the club's president called unreal for a side that was in the third division until 2021 [5]. Fabregas described the tie as a historic day [4]. Three days later, the same players host Sassuolo in Serie A.

That is the tension of this fixture. Como are fifth with 14 points from seven games and come off the best result of their season. Sassuolo are 13th with eight points and arrive off a defeat at Frosinone. But Sassuolo have had eight days since that match, and Como have had three since theirs.

Italian media report that Sassuolo have dropped five places in the table during October [7]. For a club that started the month higher, this trip is about stopping that slide. For Como, it is about proving the European high does not cost them in the league.

Results that do not quite match the chances

Como's 4-1 over United came with 2.12 expected goals against 1.11, so the scoreline was bigger than the chances but built on real control. Their other recent results are less tidy. They won 3-1 at Fiorentina while creating 1.59 and allowing 2.11, and lost 2-3 at Feyenoord while creating more than their hosts, 1.34 against 0.67. Their last five have produced 1.7 expected goals for and 1.43 against per game.

Sassuolo's numbers lean the other way. They have scored nine and conceded eleven in their last five, an average of four goals a match, with 1.44 expected goals for and 1.55 against. Their one win in that run, 3-2 at home to Juventus, came from just 1.02 expected goals. The 2-4 at Frosinone was closer in chances, 1.92 to 2.08, but the defensive record is the clear weak point.

Both squads short at the back

Como lose two players who have been on the pitch almost every week. Defender T. Chalobah has 644 minutes this season, and attacker A. Diao has 536 minutes with four goals and one assist, the best attacking return in the squad. Fabregas confirmed that Rodriguez faces a long spell out, while Assane will return after the international break [3]. M. Kean is also missing.

Sassuolo's absences land where they can least afford them. F. Leysen has played 492 minutes in seven games and is out with a knee injury, and J. Idzes is also missing. Italian media reported a defensive alarm at Sassuolo before this trip, describing players as available but their readiness uncertain [8]. They also report a demanding week, with Lazio at home in midweek and Fiorentina the following Monday [9].

Favourites, but by a little less

Across the 10 bookmakers compared, with the margin removed, Como are 61.5% to win, the draw 21.2% and Sassuolo 17.3%. The biggest 1x2 move is a 5.0-point drop for Como, and the fair line has moved from -1.28 to -1.07. That gap is consistent with the table: fifth against 13th, 14 points against eight, and a home ground that just saw a 4-1 Champions League win over Manchester United (espn.com) [1]. The line is -1, which asks Como to win by more than a single goal, and the market rates them about one goal better than Sassuolo rather than more.

That rating has come in this week. Como's win chance has slipped from 66.4% to 61.5%, while Sassuolo's has risen from 13.2% to 17.3%, and the handicap line moved from -1.28 to -1.07. The shift is consistent with the rest days, three against eight, and with Como's European midweek. The goals line is 3.25, with Over at 49.1% and both teams to score at 60.3%, which fits two teams whose recent matches have been open at both ends.

A tired favourite against a leaky guest

This is a match between a team playing better than its opponent and a team better placed to run for ninety minutes. Como have the quality, the home crowd and the confidence of Tuesday night, but they lost their most productive attacker for this game and they turn around on three days. Sassuolo have the freshness and the pace in the game to trouble a stretched side, but they have conceded eleven goals in five matches and lost a first-choice defender.

The market holds both ideas at once: Como are clear favourites, more than three times as likely to win as Sassuolo, but the handicap came in and the goals line sits above three. A high-scoring Como win is the most likely single picture, and it is not the only one.
