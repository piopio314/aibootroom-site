A bad Sunday, a European table

Juventus lost 0-3 at home to Lazio on Sunday, and most of their fans had already left the stadium before the end [4]. Four days later the same ground hosts Rennes in the Europa League. Spalletti did not hide afterwards: he said his team lacked tempo, ideas, running and the desire to work hard [2].

The context makes that night stranger. In Europe, Juventus have won both games, scored five against NEC Nijmegen, and lead the 36-team table with six points and a goal difference of +6. Rennes are 13th with four points from two matches. So Thursday is a chance to answer a bad Sunday, and also a night where another flat performance would bring more noise.

Spalletti said in his pre-match press conference that he had listened to his players in a meeting and had confirmation of their availability and honesty [3]. Italian outlets report that the club will stand by him for now, with frustration growing among supporters [5]. Gianluigi Buffon's view, in the same media, is that the coach deserves time and the problem cannot only be him [6].

Juventus give up little, Rennes give up a lot

Juventus' last five read three wins, one draw and one defeat, with ten goals scored and five conceded. They give up 0.69 expected goals per game across that run, against Rennes' 2.16. Juventus are 60.7% to win, the draw 22.0% and Rennes 17.3%. That is more than three times Rennes' chance, and it comes after a 0-3 home defeat to Lazio (football-italia.net) [1]. The main handicap line is Juventus -1: the market rates them about one goal the better side, so a 1-0 home win would only be level on that line, and a two-goal win is needed to be ahead of it.

Rennes are the opposite picture. They average 2.21 expected goals created and 2.16 against, so both boxes have been busy all autumn, and they have conceded ten goals in five matches. The damage came at Lyon (0-4) and at Paris FC (3-4), a game with four converted penalties [9]. But they also beat Auxerre 4-1 and OFI 2-1. They score even when they lose, and they lose heavily when the game opens up.

Lepaul's ankle and Juventus' wide areas

Juventus are without Francisco Conceição, who has played 525 minutes in nine appearances this season with a goal and an assist, because of a shoulder injury. Behind him on the list are Locatelli (knee, 270 minutes, one assist, a rating of 7.53) and the goalkeeper Grabara (knee). Durmisi is out after a red card and Thuram has no minutes this season. Tuttosport reported that the final session before Rennes featured a small-sided game and a double defence [8].

Rennes' news matters more. Estéban Lepaul has seven goals in nine appearances and 719 minutes this season, and he went off injured late at Paris FC after scoring a penalty hat-trick [10]. L'Équipe reports that Rennes doubt he can play, with a quote saying they do not see how he could feature [11]. Off the pitch, Foot01 reports that Haise keeps his support while sporting director Loïc Désiré is under criticism [12], and Riolo said on RMC that Haise no longer controls anything at the club [13].
