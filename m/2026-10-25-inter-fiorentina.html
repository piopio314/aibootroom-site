Top of the table against the bottom three

Inter come into Sunday at the top of Serie A: seven games, six wins, one draw, 19 points and a goal difference of +12. Fiorentina come in 18th, with four points from seven games and five defeats. At San Siro, that is the shape of the match before a ball is kicked.

Fiorentina's week made it harder. They lost 3-1 at home to Como on Sunday [7], their second straight league defeat, and conceded five goals across those two losses. Before the game Paolo Vanoli asked his team to 'learn from our mistakes and start better' [8]; the mistakes came anyway.

Inter's week was awkward but ended well. They beat Shakhtar Donetsk 1-0 in the Champions League with ten men, after Alessandro Bastoni was sent off for a second yellow card in the 68th minute [2]. The one shadow is the captain: Lautaro Martinez missed that game with a hamstring strain, according to Yahoo Sports [3].

Inter create chances worth close to three goals a game

Inter average 2.76 expected goals across their last five matches and have scored 12 goals in them. The numbers do not come only from weak opponents: they created 3.16 at Bologna in a 3-1 win and 2.86 away at Roma in a 2-2 draw. The strange night was the 3-4 home defeat to Club Brugge, a game with 4.33 expected goals for Inter and 4.29 against — the chances were there, the defending was not.

Fiorentina's profile is different. Their 1.84 expected goals per game is respectable, but away days have been quiet (0.82 at Genoa) and they have conceded eight goals in five. They have had seven days to prepare, four more than Inter, who played on Wednesday. Inter have won the last two meetings at San Siro, 3-0 and 2-1; the most recent game between the sides, in Florence in March, finished 1-1.

Lautaro a doubt, a reported goalkeeper change, Sottil out

Lautaro Martinez is Inter's captain and main striker, and a hamstring strain, reported by Yahoo Sports [3], is the kind of news that changes how a team attacks. Without him in midweek, Inter created 1.02 expected goals and won 1-0. Fiorentina's fresher legs and the question over Lautaro are the two things the game turns on.

There is also a reported change behind him: Gazzetta says Inter plan to use Provedel in Serie A, starting with this game, after Josep Martinez played against Shakhtar [4]. For Fiorentina, Sottil is out with a back injury, Moise Kean missed the Como match according to Football Italia [9], and OneFootball reports Arthur Atta and Ranieri could be fit [10]. Kean would matter most: Fiorentina have scored once in each of their last three league games.

Inter near 71%, and a line near a goal and a half

The doubt is in attack, not in the table

Inter's league position and their chance creation sit together with a 71.3% home chance and a total above three goals; Fiorentina's four points and one win explain the 12.0%. The question the market has not answered is who leads the Inter attack if Lautaro cannot start, and whether Kean is back for a Fiorentina side that has scored once in each of its last three league games. Inter are clear favourites at San Siro, the goals line says the game should be open, and Fiorentina's fresher legs are the away side's main argument.
