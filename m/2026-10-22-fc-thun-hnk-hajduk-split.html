The Ajax conqueror comes to Thun

Hajduk Split arrive in Thun on the back of a win over Ajax. They beat the Dutch side 2-1 at home on 15 October in their Conference League opener, and Blick now calls them the "Ajax conqueror" [1].

Thun had a very different start. They lost 0-1 at Twente on the same night, they sit 30th in the 36-team table with 0 points, and they have won only 1 of their last 5 matches in all competitions. The question tonight is whether the hosts can get off the mark at home.

Thun need a result soon. Bluewin describes Hajduk as the leader of the Croatian league, and says that after this match Thun host the leader of the Swiss Super League [2][8]. Two hard home games in a row make a first point tonight even more important.

Wins and good chances against a leaky defence

Hajduk have won 4 of their last 5 matches, scoring 6 goals and conceding 5. The only defeat came at Rijeka, 0-3, on 20 September. The Ajax win was earned on chances: Hajduk created 1.99 expected goals, a measure of the quality of chances, against 1.15 for Ajax. They then beat Varazdin 1-0 at home on 18 October.

Thun's story is the reverse. At Twente they created only 0.2 expected goals while Twente created 2.32, so the 0-1 defeat fitted the play. Over their last five matches Thun average 1.35 expected goals for and 1.89 against, and they conceded 12 goals, including a 2-4 loss at Zurich on 10 October. They drew 2-2 at Schaffhausen in the Swiss Cup on 18 October. Both teams played four days ago, so rest is no factor.

Coach and players talk, police prepare

The talk before kick-off is about expectations. Croatian journalists see Hajduk as the favourites, but Thun coach Privitelli disagrees, according to Blick [3]. Thun player Nicolas Bürgy is more careful: Blick reports that he says even a draw against the Ajax conqueror would be worth a lot [4]. Both remarks are press quotes, as Blick reports them.

Away from the pitch, the match is rated a risk game. According to 20 Minuten, Bern cantonal police will be visibly present in Thun, after Hajduk fans chanted pro-Nazi slogans before the Ajax game [5]. Sportstar reports that Croatian leaders condemned the chants and that police arrested at least a dozen fans [6]. Blick adds that Thun are carrying out special checks on their own fans [7].

A modest lead for the visitors

The market agrees with the Croatian journalists. Across the 10 bookmakers we compare, with the margin removed, Hajduk have a 41.2% chance to win, Thun 32.9% and the draw 25.9%. Hajduk are favourites, but the gap is not wide. The handicap, which is a head start in goals, shows the same picture: Thun get a head start of a quarter of a goal, so the market rates the two sides close together.

Prices moved a little in recent days. Hajduk's win chance rose from 39.9% to 41.2%, a small move that is consistent with their win over Ajax and Thun's slow start. Thun's own chance eased by 1.0 point. The main goals line is 3.0, and the chance of more goals than that is 48.6%, which points to an open game. That fits Thun's 12 goals conceded in five matches. The chance that both teams score is 62.5%.

Form against need

This is a match between form and need. Hajduk bring the better results and the stronger defensive record (1.12 expected goals conceded a match, against Thun's 1.89), plus the market's support, but only by a modest margin. Thun's coach has disagreed with the view that Hajduk are favourites [3]. Thun need a first point, and with equal rest and an open game in the market's picture, the hosts have room to get one.
