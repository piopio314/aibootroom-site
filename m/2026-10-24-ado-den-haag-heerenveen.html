One win, one wave

ADO Den Haag's season has one bright page, and it is the most recent one. They beat PSV 2-1 at home on Saturday, a result Yahoo Sports described as stunning [1], and it is their only league win of the campaign. They are still 17th of 18, with six points from nine games and a goal difference of -9.

Heerenveen arrive with the opposite feeling. They opened the round with a 4-0 home win over Excelsior [6], sit eighth on 12 points and, according to Omroepwest, want to close the gap to the subtop [9]. A trip to the team in 17th is exactly the fixture a side with that ambition should take.

The mood in The Hague is level rather than giddy. Club icon Lex Schoenmaker said after the PSV win that "everyone beats almost everyone" and stayed happy but calm [2]. That is a fair reading of a team that has won once in nine.

The chances tell a different story

ADO's last five bring seven goals scored and nine conceded, but the chance numbers are harsher. They average 0.96 xG created and 1.82 conceded, so they are out-created by nearly a goal a match. Even the PSV win was narrow on chances: 1.43 xG to 1.07.

Their points have come from heavy defending. They conceded 2.86 xG at Telstar on 11 October and still drew 2-2, and 2.81 at Twente in a 0-2 defeat. Heerenveen's five-game profile is cleaner — 1.84 xG created and 1.1 allowed, with 2.8 against 0.72 in the Excelsior win. Both sides had a full week: seven rest days for ADO, eight for Heerenveen.

New faces, new deals

ADO's week has been about building the squad. The club confirmed the signing of 18-year-old forward Eternal Barron on a contract to summer 2027 after he left Ajax as a free agent [4], and 16-year-old right winger Joshairo Castelen, who arrived from Heerenveen, until mid-2028 [5].

Heerenveen's news is about keeping people. Before kick-off against Excelsior the club announced that Bernt Klaverboer had extended to mid-2029 and Nolhan Courtens to mid-2030 [8], and Klaverboer called that 4-0 evening one he will not soon forget, "maybe even never again" [7]. Robin Peter, meanwhile, can chase a second straight win with ADO, according to Omroepwest [3].

The PSV route

The two stories of last weekend pull in different directions, and the football explains why the market followed one of them. ADO's win over PSV was real, but a side creating 0.96 xG a game and conceding 1.82 needs a scrappy match with few chances to repeat it. Heerenveen's 4-0 was built on 2.8 xG, and their five-game numbers say it was not a one-off. So the picture is a top-half side with something to chase visiting the team in 17th, rated about 0.4 goals better and given a 47.6% chance. ADO's route is the one they used against PSV — stay in the game, defend the box, take the moment — and the market makes that the smaller of the two outcomes at 27.6%.
