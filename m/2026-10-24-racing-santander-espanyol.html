Two eight-point sides, one level line

El Sardinero hosts two clubs separated by nothing in the table and a lot in the numbers. Racing Santander and Espanyol both have eight points from nine games in La Liga. The market makes the home side narrow favourites anyway, even though Racing have conceded chances worth 3.26 expected goals per game across their last five matches — more than double Espanyol's 1.27.

Espanyol arrive four games without a win. Their last match was a 0-1 home defeat to Atletico Madrid, with Alex Baena scoring the winner according to beIN Sports [7]. A late goal turned a point into another defeat, and the run now includes a 1-3 home loss to Elche and a 1-2 at Rayo Vallecano.

The situation is simple. The lower half of the table is tight, and a win here puts a little distance between a club and the relegation conversation. According to lagrada.org, Espanyol are one point above the relegation zone [6]. Racing, with a goal difference of -12, are not comfortable either.

Racing's open games, Espanyol's tight ones

Racing's last five have been wild. They lost 0-2 at Osasuna, creating 0.28 xG and allowing 3.65, having earlier been beaten 2-7 at Barcelona and 0-5 at Celta Vigo. At home they have been far more competitive: a 2-1 win over Alaves and a 2-2 draw with Valencia. Six goals scored and 17 conceded across five matches tells you what kind of team this is right now.

Espanyol's numbers are calmer. Five goals scored and seven conceded in five, one win in that run, and a season goal difference of -1 against Racing's -12. Both clubs carry the same form string, L-D-L-L-W, but the two runs look very different on the pitch. Espanyol had seven days since their last match, Racing six.

A squad player's knock, and a striker carrying the team

Racing's team news is thin. Defender Pedro Felipe left training early with a knock three days before kick-off, reported by infobae.com [1]. He has made three league appearances this season, all from the bench, according to rotowire.com [2], so a possible absence should not change how Racing set up at the back.

The bigger question is at the other end. Yassir Zabiri has scored seven goals in eight La Liga appearances, africasoccer.com reports [3], against an expected-goals total of just 3.3. That is finishing well beyond what his team creates, and it is a large part of why Racing can be favoured while conceding so much. Goalkeeper Julen Agirrezabala made five saves in the last match but conceded the goals that decided it, rotowire.com reports [4].
