Nine points apart, almost level in the price

Cardiff host Birmingham on Saturday afternoon with the table and the market saying different things. Birmingham are 4th with 20 points from eleven games. Cardiff are 19th with 11. Yet the market makes Birmingham only narrow favourites: 38.4% against Cardiff's 36.2%, with the draw at 25.4%. Nine points separate them, and the price says almost nothing does.

Cardiff's weekend explains why they are still respected at home. They led 2-0 at Middlesbrough, a side chasing promotion, and drew 2-2 after Osho was sent off [3]. BBC Sport noted that the draw began a hard run of fixtures which continues with Birmingham's visit [4]. Cardiff have two wins from eleven games and five draws, so they stay in matches rather than killing them.

Birmingham arrive on a five-game unbeaten run, with 12 goals scored and three conceded in those matches [7]. Liam Millar scored twice in the 3-0 home win over QPR. BBC Radio 5 Live reporter Adam Cottier wrote that Chris Davies's side are evolving this season [9] — and the results are following the change.

What the chances say about the results

Cardiff's expected goals numbers are uneven. At Middlesbrough they created 0.91 and allowed 2.78, so the point owed a lot to the home side's finishing. Against Watford they won 5-1 with 3.67 xG. Over the five games they average 2.0 for and 1.68 against, which fits a team that creates enough and gives away enough to draw rather than win. Their two defeats came from opposite problems: out-created at Blackburn, and beaten 0-1 at Bolton after creating 1.79 to 0.48.

Birmingham average 2.1 xG for and 1.19 against across the same five matches. The QPR win was efficient rather than dominant — 1.3 xG for a 3-0 scoreline — but the 4-0 over Preston (2.24 to 0.49) and the 1-0 at West Brom (2.55 to 1.05) were controlled from start to finish. Both teams had seven days between games, so neither arrives short of rest.

History on one side, form on the other

Everything points one way and then stops. Form, table, goals scored and the direction of the price all favour Birmingham. History, home advantage and Cardiff's habit of staying in games hold the fixture level. Cardiff have won four of the last five meetings between the clubs, including 1-0 at Birmingham in April 2024. That is why a side nine points better off is not a clear favourite here, and why the level handicap is the honest picture of the match. The read is a close game with goals in it: Birmingham are the favourites, Cardiff are far from out of it, and the two chances sit within two points of each other.
