A favourite with a strange record

Thursday night in Farum puts 17th against 28th in the Conference League table, with the favourite carrying a recent record that does not read like one. Nordsjaelland have won once in five, drawing three, and arrive from a 3-3 at Lyngby in which they were 3-0 down. CSKA Sofia have won three of five and scored in every one of them.

Both sides know where they stand. Nordsjaelland have one point from their opener, a 1-1 draw at Hearts, as Spilxperten reported [2], and were out-created 1.81 to 0.64 on expected goals. CSKA have none, after losing 1-2 at home to Monaco in a game where the chance count was almost level, 1.28 to 1.32.

That is the tension of the night. The hosts make chances and drop points; the visitors take points and arrive as heavy outsiders. Sunday showed both faces of Nordsjaelland at once: three goals in six minutes to rescue the draw at Lyngby, and a missed penalty in the last seconds [1].
