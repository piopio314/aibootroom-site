Augsburg favoured to end winless run against goal-shy Union

A table that points the other way

Augsburg have gone four matches without a win, and on Saturday they host a Union Berlin team sitting 17th in the Bundesliga. Welt reported this week that this is the game Augsburg need a result from, ideally [1]. The table points one way and the recent form points another, and that gap is the whole story of this fixture.

Union's season is the reason Augsburg are favoured. The visitors are 17th with 5 points from six games and a goal difference of -12, and they have scored twice in their last five Bundesliga matches. Even so, they have one win and two draws from six, so their season is not without points.

For Augsburg the pressure is simple: 10th place, 8 points, and a home game against a side in the relegation zone. The club's week has been about the occasion as much as the points — the 'Augsburg Calling 2026' programme brings both sets of fans together from Friday, the Augsburg Journal reports [11].

A drift, not a collapse

The winless run is a slide rather than a fall. Augsburg drew 2-2 with Bayern München and 2-2 with Bayer Leverkusen, then lost at Werder Bremen and 4-3 at Elversberg, a game sportschau.de reported as a seven-goal thriller [2]. Their one win in five was a 4-1 at Eintracht Frankfurt.

The expected-goals numbers say more than the results. Augsburg's last five games have produced 21 goals, and they allow 3.28 expected goals against per game, including 4.96 in the draw with Bayern. Union's run is the opposite picture: 0-0 with Dortmund, a 1-0 win over Elversberg, then 0-7 at Bayern, 0-4 at Leverkusen and 1-3 at home to Schalke, at 0.92 expected goals for per game. Both sides have had seven days since they last played.

A return, not an absence

The team news here is about a player coming back rather than one missing. Felix Uduokhai, who spent five years at Augsburg, returns to the WWK Arena with Union, and the Augsburg Journal reports he has been key to their recent defensive stabilisation [7]. Union have conceded twice in their last two matches after shipping seven at Bayern and four at Leverkusen earlier in the run.

Jeffrey Gouweleeuw could set a club record for Augsburg on Saturday, having already matched Daniel Baier's mark, according to the Augsburg Journal [3]. Both clubs also play DFB-Pokal second-round ties next week — Augsburg against Sonnenhof Großaspach [5], Union against 1. FC Köln [10] — which is the only real team-selection question hanging over the weekend.

Half a goal, and three of them

Across the six bookmakers we compare, Augsburg are 51.3% to win, the draw 24.0% and Union 24.7% — Union's chance is less than half of Augsburg's. The handicap line is -0.5 and the fair line sits at -0.57, so the market rates Augsburg about half a goal better rather than a full goal. That is a modest gap for a home side against a team 17th in the table, and it fits Augsburg's own four-match winless run.

The total-goals line is 3.0, with Over at 51.0%, and both teams to score is priced at 62.4%. That last number is the interesting one: Union have two goals in five matches, yet the market leans towards them finding the net, because Augsburg have conceded in each of their last five.

The tension the price cannot settle

Winless they may be, Augsburg are the clear home favourites, and the reason is Union's season rather than Augsburg's own form. The half-goal handicap says a one-goal margin is the likely shape of the game; the 3.0 goals line and the both-teams-to-score price say it should be an open one, and Augsburg's expected goals against is the number behind that. What the market cannot settle is the tension at the heart of it — a team that cannot win hosting a team that cannot score. Augsburg's chance is more likely than not, which is not the same as certain.
