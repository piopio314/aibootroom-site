Two teams on zero, one night to fix it

Celtic and Celta Vigo reach Thursday with exactly the same problem. Both have lost both of their Europa League games, both sit on zero points, and neither has anything to show from the league phase so far [8]. Celtic are 33rd of 36 with a goal difference of -5; Celta are 28th with -2.

That is why this feels bigger than a third group game. Another European defeat at home would leave Celtic with almost no room to reach the knockout places, and Celta know a win would put them level with the teams chasing those places. Their supporters are travelling to Glasgow in record numbers, according to the Daily Record [11].

Celta forward Ferran Jutgla called it a hugely important fixture for both clubs [7]. Celtic arrive off a 7-1 win over Hearts, their biggest scoring day of the season [3]. The question is whether that form crosses from the Premiership into Europe, where Celtic have conceded seven goals in two matches.

A 7-1 win, and seven goals let in

Celtic arrive off a 7-1 win over Hearts, sports.yahoo.com reports [3], a result that underlined their attacking threat — they average 2.28 xG across their last five games. But the European games tell another story: they lost 1-4 at Benfica and their xG against average over five games is 1.66.

Celta's numbers lean the other way. They beat Alaves 2-1 on Sunday but were out-created, 0.97 xG to 1.06, and they average 1.17 xG over their last five — less than half Celtic's figure. Their two Europa League defeats, 0-1 at Omonia and 1-2 at home to Juventus, were each by a single goal. Both teams had four days' rest, so freshness is not a difference between them.

Our read

Celtic have the attack to win this and the defence to lose it. Celta create little — 1.17 xG a game — and arrive with a first-choice midfielder missing and a doubt in Duran, but they have lost both European matches by one goal. The market's answer is a level line and near-identical chances, and the football agrees: two teams with the same record and different weaknesses.

Celtic's crowd and their 2.28 xG give them the better route to the win. Their European defence is the reason the market refuses to make them favourites at home, and the level handicap is the honest summary of that.
