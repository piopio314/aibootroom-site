A homecoming after Levante

Athletic Club return to San Mamés on Sunday with something to answer. Four days ago they lost 3-1 at Levante, their first defeat at that ground since 2018 [2]. The coach, Terzić, said it plainly afterwards: his side must stop giving away silly balls in the middle of the pitch, blaming two midfield losses for the defeat [3].

The table adds to the pressure. Both clubs have 9 points, but Athletic have played seven games to Getafe's nine, so Athletic sit 11th with games in hand and Getafe 14th. Three points on Sunday would give their season some shape; another home draw would leave a good side standing still.

Getafe, though, have the better recent record in this fixture. They won 1-0 at San Mamés in October 2025 and 2-0 at home in April 2026, so the last two meetings went their way. That sits awkwardly against Athletic's status as strong favourites, and it is the reason this is not a simple afternoon.

Chances created, goals missing

Athletic's last five league games read one win, three draws and one defeat. They lost to Levante, drew with Valencia, Alaves and Elche, and beat Atletico Madrid 3-0 at home in early September. The chance data explains the frustration: expected goals, the quality of the chances a team creates, was 2.39 to 0.84 against Elche and the game ended 1-1; it was 1.62 to 0.05 against Alaves and that ended 0-0. They create; they do not finish.

Getafe's numbers point the other way. They average 0.69 expected goals per game across their last five, against 1.44 conceded. At Barcelona they lost 0-3 with 0.14 xG; at Betis they lost 0-1 with 0.29. Their away league games in this run produced no goals at all. They are also the fresher team: six days of rest to Athletic's four, because Athletic's extra fixture was the Levante defeat.

Williams back, Sancet doubtful

The best news of the week for the home side is Nico Williams, back in group training and back in the squad after injury, according to Yahoo Sports [1]. Athletic have scored five goals in their last five league games, and the width and one-against-one threat he brings was missing in the home draws with Alaves and Valencia. The doubt is Oihan Sancet, who needs more time and whose availability for Sunday is doubtful, reported by Rotowire [4]. Sancet's absence would leave the central creative role thinner and put more weight on Williams' return.

Getafe have a returning body of their own: Ivan Azon is back in full training after discomfort, reported by Rotowire, which gives them another attacking option [7]. Off the pitch, AS reported that Dani Carvajal has joined the club after leaving Real Madrid and started in his debut [8]. Both stories add interest to a Getafe squad that has scored three goals in its last five matches.

Three times the chance, one goal better

The bookmakers we compare, with the margin removed, make Athletic 58.8% to win, the draw 23.7% and Getafe 17.5%. That is more than three times Getafe's chance, and the reason is their away record: two away league games in this run, at Barcelona and at Betis, and no goals scored in either.

The handicap tells the same story at a smaller scale. The main line is -0.75, and the fair line sits at -0.85, up from -0.83 earlier in the week. In plain terms, the market rates Athletic about 0.85 goals better than Getafe — a narrow home win, not a rout, which fits a season of single-goal wins and home draws. The goals line has slipped too: 2.25 is the main line with Over at 46.4%, and the fair line fell from 2.2 to 2.14. The moves were small — Over 2.25 down 1.8 points, both teams to score 'yes' down 1.8, Getafe's win chance down 1.6 — all consistent with a tight, low-scoring home win.

Our read

Sunday's picture is a wounded team that is still trusted, against an opponent that creates very little and arrives fresher. The market expects an Athletic win, by a narrow, low-scoring margin: 58.8% to Getafe's 17.5%, on a goals line of 2.25. The risk sits in Athletic's own season, because their favouritism at Levante did not survive contact with the match, and a Getafe side that has won the last two meetings will be happy to keep everything tight. More likely than not a home win — not a comfortable afternoon.
