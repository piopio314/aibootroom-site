Same one point, very different moods

Two teams with the same record meet in Norway on Thursday. Real Sociedad are 22nd of 36 in the Europa League with one point, and Lillestrom are 23rd with one point. Neither has won a game in the league phase, which makes this a night for points rather than patience.

Sociedad's route here is the stranger one. They lost 1-2 at home to Bournemouth, drew 1-1 at Union St. Gilloise, and then won 5-2 at Malaga on Sunday. Thirteen goals in their last five official matches, according to Eurosport [7], is a lot of scoring for a side with one point from two European games — the problem has been the other end.

Lillestrom's story is different. They won the 2025 Norwegian cup in December while still a second-division club [3], and Hans Erik Odegaard, Martin's father, took them up and then to that trophy, according to Diario Vasco [2]. Arasen Stadion has an away section [6], and Sociedad fans are expected in Oslo [9].

Wins that hide the chances given away

Sunday looked good for both sides, and the xG says less. Lillestrom won 2-0 at Sandefjord with 0.51 xG for and 1.35 against — two goals from very little, and plenty allowed. Sociedad's 5-2 at Malaga was built on 2.44 xG for and 1.24 against, a big scoreline from a controlled but not overwhelming performance.

The pattern repeats for the home side. Lillestrom gave up 2.95 xG at home to Molde in a 2-2 and 2.98 at Viking in a 0-3, and their average xG against across the last five is 2.10. Sociedad average 1.82 xG for and 1.71 against, and they conceded in all five of those matches — including twice at Malaga. Both teams had four days between games, so freshness is level.

Herrera doubt reaches the middle of the pitch

Sociedad's midfield is the question. Y. Herrera, their most-used player this season with 622 minutes in 10 appearances, is doubtful with a calf problem, and right-back J. Aramburu is out with a leg injury after 597 minutes of his own. L. Beitia is also doubtful with an ankle issue, so both wide areas of the defence could look different.

Lillestrom are without attacker C. Jebara (329 minutes, one goal this season) and defender E. B. Garnas, who has a knee injury. Neither is a guaranteed starter, but a squad of this size feels two absences more than a big club would. The market has drifted a touch toward Sociedad: the fair handicap line moved from 1.04 to 1.09 goals in their favour. Lillestrom's win chance eased 0.5 percentage points, a flat move.

A one-goal edge, not a rout

The Malaga version, or the Bournemouth one

The through-line is a good team playing badly in Europe against a promoted side that keeps giving up chances. Sociedad's one point and their 5-2 at Malaga pull in opposite directions, and the market has chosen the Malaga version, rating them about a goal the better side. Lillestrom's route to a result is the same as it was against Molde: score from little and hope the chances against do not turn into three goals. Sociedad are clear favourites, but the line says tight, not comfortable.
