A favourite that cannot win

Gaziantep FK have lost their last three Süper Lig matches and go into their next home game against Erzurumspor FK below their visitors in the table. The market, though, still makes them favourites at 41.8% — more than one and a half times Erzurumspor's 25.9%. That gap between the table and the price is the whole story of this match.

The week around the club has been heavy. Gaziantep have called an extraordinary elective general assembly for 5 November at Gaziantep Büyükşehir Stadium, confirmed by sondakika.com [1]. Local media reported that the vote was called over financial difficulties and a lack of expected support, according to olaymedya.com [2].

None of that changes the eleven that Orhan Ak can put on the pitch. Training continued with a 1 hour 10 minute session of passing drills and an in-house match [3], and the club is pushing for a fuller ground, giving students 5,000 free tickets for this game [7]. A loud home crowd is exactly the factor the price is built on.

Three defeats, and the numbers behind them

The run reads LLLDW. Since a 4-2 win at Göztepe in early September, Gaziantep have drawn 0-0 at home to Fenerbahçe and then lost three in a row: 0-2 at Kocaelispor, 0-3 at home to Çorum FK and 1-2 at Başakşehir last Sunday [4]. The numbers behind those results are unkind — 2.1 expected goals allowed per game across the last five, and 2.67 in the Çorum defeat alone, against only 1.16 created.

Erzurumspor arrive from the other direction: WDWLW, three wins in five, and just three goals conceded in that span. All three wins came at home — 1-0 against Konyaspor, 1-0 against Samsunspor and 2-0 against Eyüpspor last Saturday, with headed goals from Cardoso and Eren Tozlu [8]. Away from home they drew 0-0 at Alanyaspor, where they created more than the hosts (1.69 xG to 0.98), and lost 0-3 at Beşiktaş [11]. Four goals in five matches explains why the market keeps them as outsiders, and the rest days are almost level: six for Gaziantep, seven for Erzurumspor.

The table backs the visitors: 11 points from eight games and 11th place, three points and three places above Gaziantep, who are 14th on 8. The goal differences are close, -6 against -4, in a crowded bottom half.

Two names out, a congress in the background

Gaziantep's absentee list is short and does not cut deep. F. Bavuk, a forward with 54 minutes across two appearances this season, is out with a hamstring injury, and N. Sangare is also listed as out with a back injury. The squad available to Orhan Ak is close to what he has had in recent weeks, so the problems here are about confidence more than personnel.

Erzurumspor report no absentees. Their coach Serkan Özbalta said after the Eyüpspor win that his side is "after every match and every point" [9]. The mood is not perfect: Turkish media reported heavy criticism of the club's new signing despite that 2-0 win, according to gazetevatan.com [12]. Both clubs also have administrative noise — the TFF referred Erzurumspor to its disciplinary board, as reported by milliyet.com.tr [10], and referred Orhan Ak, reported by nefes.com.tr [5]. Ajansspor separately reported that Gaziantep are among the Süper Lig clubs under a transfer registration ban over a FIFA file [6]. None of it touches Saturday's starting eleven.

A quarter of a goal, and a line at 2.25

The win chances come from the ten bookmakers we compare, with their margin removed: Gaziantep 41.8%, the draw 32.4%, Erzurumspor 25.9%. Read plainly, this is favouritism built on venue rather than dominance — the home side is ahead, and the draw takes a large share of the rest. The Asian handicap says the same in goals: at -0.25, Gaziantep are rated about a quarter of a goal better, the smallest separation a line can carry. It describes two close teams, with home advantage given the deciding weight.

The goals line is 2.25, with over at 50.9%, and both teams to score sits at 50.4% — a game the market places right on the edge of two and three goals. That fits two records pulling against each other. Gaziantep's recent matches have been open, with nine goals conceded in five and 2.1 expected goals allowed per game. Erzurumspor's have been tight: three conceded in five, and 1.12 expected goals allowed per game.

Despite a three-match losing run and a congress call at the club, covered by www.sondakika.com [1], Gaziantep are 41.8% favourites: ahead of the draw and more than one and a half times Erzurumspor's 25.9%, with the handicap at a quarter of a goal and the goals line at 2.25.

Home advantage is the whole argument

The football and the price meet in one place: the venue. Erzurumspor have won three of five and every one of those wins came at home; away they drew at Alanyaspor and lost at Beşiktaş. Gaziantep have not won since early September and give up 2.1 expected goals a game, but they are at home, in front of a crowd the club is working to fill. That is why the handicap stops at a quarter of a goal and the goals line stops at 2.25 — neither side has earned a bigger rating. The 41.8% rests on home advantage and on an Erzurumspor attack that has managed four goals in five games, not on Gaziantep's form. If the home defence keeps leaking chances at the same rate, that favouritism will be tested; if the crowd the club is trying to fill turns up, the home edge the price rests on is the one thing Erzurumspor have to overcome.
