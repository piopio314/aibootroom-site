A derby the form book cannot settle

The Black Sea derby sends Trabzonspor along the coast to Rizespor on Monday 26 October 2026, and they arrive with a four-match winning run behind them, according to Daily Sabah [4]. The run includes a 2-0 home win over Beşiktaş, with Mohamed Salah scoring the second goal, according to BBC [1]. Rizespor, at home, have won once in five and sit twelfth.

That form is why the bookmakers make the away side the favourite in a derby. Trabzonspor are 45.3% to win, against 29.6% for Rizespor and 25.1% for the draw. It is a clear lean rather than a wide one: the home side keeps close to a third of the chance.

For Rizespor this is the visit of their neighbours from Trabzon, and home form is the soft part of their autumn. They lost 0-3 at home to Fenerbahçe and 0-1 at home to Alanyaspor in their last five. Their own ground is the obvious place to answer that, against Trabzonspor, who have won four of the last five meetings between them.

Rizespor's xG and Trabzonspor's run

Rizespor's run reads one win, one draw and three defeats, and the expected goals say the results are not simply bad luck. Against Fenerbahçe they created more than they allowed, 1.52 expected goals to 0.96, and lost 0-3. Across the five games they average 1.29 expected goals for and 1.59 against, so they are being out-created as well as out-scored. Ten days have passed since their last match, a 2-1 defeat to Çorum FK.

Trabzonspor's numbers are stronger in every direction. They average 2.21 expected goals across their last five matches and have scored 13 goals in five, while giving up chances at 1.40 expected goals against. Those five games produced 16 goals in total, which is the background to a high goals line here. They are fourth with 16 points and a goal difference of +13; Rizespor are twelfth on 10 points at -3. Trabzonspor have had seven days since Beşiktaş.

Onuachu doubtful, Salah scoring

The team news that matters is in Trabzonspor's attack. Thomas Reis said, according to 24saatgazetesi.com, that Paul Onuachu and Mustafa Eskihellaç will not feature against Hearts, and that Onuachu is doubtful for the Rizespor match [2]. Onuachu is the target man who holds the ball while the team moves up the pitch, so if he misses out Trabzonspor lose their reference point in a derby away from home.

The rest of the picture points the other way for Trabzonspor: Mohamed Salah tops the Süper Lig scoring charts with eight goals in eight games, according to Daily Sabah [5], and Stefan Savic said after the Beşiktaş win that the team can raise their level further [6].

Rizespor have prepared with passing and tactical work under Recep Uçar at their training ground [8], and defender Modibo Sagnan has spoken about the fixture [7]. No absences of note are listed for either side beyond the Trabzonspor pair.

A third of a goal, three goals expected

The win chances, with the bookmakers' margin removed, are 45.3% Trabzonspor, 29.6% Rizespor and 25.1% draw. The handicap says the same thing in a different way. Rizespor carry a start of a quarter of a goal at +0.25, while the fair line puts Trabzonspor about a third of a goal better at 0.33 — so on neutral ground this would be almost level, and home advantage is doing much of the work.

Goals are expected rather than a cautious night: the line sits at 2.97, effectively three, with Over 3.0 at 49.3% and both teams to score at 62.4%. The week's moves have been small. Trabzonspor's win chance has come in about two points, the home handicap at +0.25 has firmed by 2.2 points, and the fair handicap line has shifted from 0.42 to 0.33 goals in Rizespor's favour. That is consistent with the doubt over Onuachu, and the goals line drifting the other way, from 2.89 to 2.97, fits two teams whose recent matches have been open.

What we said before Beşiktaş

A week ago we previewed Trabzonspor against Beşiktaş and called it a derby the market could not split: 38.1% home, 37.1% away. Trabzonspor won 2-0, so the slight home lean landed on the right side. We also leaned towards goals with a fair total near 3.25, and the match produced two — the clean sheet was the part we got wrong.

Our read

Trabzonspor are the side in form: four straight wins, a lot of chances created, the leading scorer in the league. The market sees all of that and still keeps the derby close, because the game is away, because Rizespor have had ten days to prepare and because Onuachu may not play. Our read follows the price: Trabzonspor are the more likely winners, but only by about a third of a goal, in a match where around three goals are expected.
