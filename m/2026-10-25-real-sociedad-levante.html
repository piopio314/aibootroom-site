Fifth against seventeenth, but not a simple Sunday

Real Sociedad start the weekend fifth in La Liga with 16 points from nine games, and they arrive on the back of a 5-2 win at Málaga on Sunday [1]. Mundo Deportivo reported that the result lifted them to fifth and left them threatening the top sides [2]; the same outlet said the display at La Rosaleda eased doubts and lowered tensions around Pellegrino's side [3].

Levante come from the other end of the table — 17th, seven points from eight games, one win all season — but not from the other end of the form book. They beat Athletic Club 3-1 at home last night in their game in hand [6], ending a run of two draws and two defeats.

That is the tension of this fixture. A top-five side at home against a bottom-half one, with the visitors arriving on the better result. And Real Sociedad's week is not finished: according to Mundo Deportivo, they travel to Lillestrøm in the Europa League on Thursday, three days before this match [4].

Real Sociedad win without controlling; Levante concede plenty

Real Sociedad's last five read W-D-W-W-L, with 13 goals scored and nine conceded, and an xG average of 1.82 for and 1.71 against. The 5-2 at Málaga came from 2.44 xG, so it was ruthless finishing rather than a flood of chances, and the 3-2 home win over Deportivo La Coruña came with 1.44 xG against 2.13 allowed. Fifth place, notably, sits on a goal difference of zero.

Levante's numbers are louder. Their last five league games produced 22 goals — 4.4 a game — and they have conceded 2.42 xG per game across that run, which means good chances against them almost every week. Even the 3-1 over Athletic was close on chances, at 2.05 xG to 1.95, so the win was real but not a sudden defensive fix.

No absentees, so the calendar is the team news

No absentees are listed for either squad, which pushes the story onto the week itself. Mundo Deportivo reports that the Europa League squad for the trip to Norway includes a new name, Alex Lebarbier [5] — one more sign that Real Sociedad are managing this week in two parts, with Thursday's away game before Levante visit.

Levante's news is brighter. Roger Brugué scored twice against Athletic to reach five league goals for the season, reported by Infobae [7] — the visitors' clearest route into a home defence that has conceded nine goals in its last five matches.

A clear home favourite, not an overwhelming one

Across the 10 bookmakers we compare, with the margin removed, Real Sociedad are 55.1% to win, with the draw at 23.1% and Levante at 21.8% — the home win is more than twice as likely as the away one. The handicap line is -0.75, with a fair line of -0.74: the market rates them about three-quarters of a goal better, so a one-goal win only half covers it and a two-goal win covers it in full. Levante's four draws in eight league games are the reason the line stops short of a full goal.

The total-goals line is 3.0, with Over at 48.1% and a fair total of 2.93 — the market leans just under three goals. Both teams to score is 57.6%, against a Levante side conceding 2.42 xG a game and a Real Sociedad defence that has shipped nine goals in its last five. The handicap at -0.75 and the 3.0 goals line both fit a game the market expects to be open but controlled by the home side.

We had Levante three days ago, and the goals beat us

Marca reported that Levante beat Athletic Club 3-1 at the Ciutat de València in their LaLiga game in hand [6], their first league win of the season, and the match produced four goals against a 2.05-1.95 xG split, so the win was real but narrow on chances. Real Sociedad's Europa League trip to Lillestrøm on Thursday, according to Mundo Deportivo [4], is now the open question over this fixture; Mundo Deportivo reports that their 5-2 win at Málaga eased doubts and lifted them to fifth [2][3].

Why the favourite is clear but not certain

Almost everything points one way: fifth against 17th, a 5-2 away win against a defence giving up 2.42 xG a game, and a home price of 55.1% that carries both facts. The argument for Levante is timing — they come off a 3-1 win, with Brugué scoring, while Real Sociedad have a Thursday flight to Norway in the middle of their week. That is why the home side is a clear favourite rather than an untouchable one, and why three-quarters of a goal, not a full one, is the fair description of this match.
