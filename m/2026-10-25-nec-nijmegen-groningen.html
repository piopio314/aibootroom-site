A favourite that cannot win

NEC Nijmegen have not won in eight matches in all competitions. On Sunday they host Groningen, a team four points and five places above them in the Eredivisie. Even so, the home side is the favourite — and that gap between the table and the price is the story of this game.

The last result was a 2-2 home draw with Fortuna Sittard, and it was not a bad performance. NEC created 3.06 xG and allowed 0.45, missed a penalty and conceded a late equaliser [1]. Dutch reports say the point did little to ease the discontent around the club [2]. Four of their last five matches have ended level.

Groningen arrive from the other direction. They are seventh with 14 points, they have scored 13 goals in their last five league games, and last weekend they lost 5-2 at home to Ajax [7]. In recent days their chance of winning this match has risen by nearly five points, to 34.6%, while NEC's has slipped by more than four, to 41.7%. This is close to a level game now.

Chances made, points dropped

NEC's run reads DDDDL: four draws and a 0-5 defeat at Juventus. The draws hide a team that is creating. They averaged 1.8 xG across that run, made 3.06 against Fortuna alone, 2.06 against Levski Sofia and 1.05 at Ajax in another 1-1. The problem is finishing games, not starting them.

Groningen average 1.61 xG and 1.95 against over the same five matches. That is consistent with the five they conceded to Ajax and with the 5-1 win at Excelsior. The 0.16 xG they managed at GO Ahead Eagles is the warning — this attack can go quiet on the road. On points Groningen are the better side; on chance quality the two teams are close.

Legs, noise and a defence under question

The clearest team news is at the back of a busy week for the hosts. Dušan Tadić has been left out of NEC's squad for the Europa League trip to Dinamo Zagreb, reported by drimble.nl [3], with Dick Schreuder's side also playing a European away game in the middle of this league week.

There is noise around the squad too. Willum Willumsson scored his first NEC goal, a late header for 2-2 against Fortuna, but reports say he is unsure about a bench role given his limited playing time [5]. Assistant coach Haris Medunjanin has left the club of his own accord, reported by gld.nl [4]. For Groningen, Dagblad van het Noorden reported that the Ajax defeat exposed defensive problems in Dick Lukkien's team [8], and Johan Derksen said on Vandaag Inside he watched the game in disbelief [9].

A quarter of a goal, and a line above three

Across the 10 bookmakers we compare, with the margin removed, NEC are 41.7% to win, the draw is 23.7% and Groningen are 34.6%. That is a narrow favourite: about seven points of probability, most of it home advantage. The handicap says the same in goals. NEC are rated a quarter of a goal better, and the fair line has come from -0.35 to -0.16, so the room between the sides has shrunk.

The goals line is 3.25, with over priced at 52.1%, and the 50/50 total has moved from 3.14 to 3.35. Both teams to score sits at 66.9%, consistent with NEC scoring and conceding in each of their last three league games and with Groningen's open run of nine conceded in five. This week's moves all point one way: Groningen up 4.7 points, NEC down 4.3, the away handicap up 4.8 and over 3.25 up 4.2 — after a weekend when NEC's run stretched to eight and Groningen shipped five at home.

What we said about Zagreb

Three days ago we wrote about NEC's Europa League trip to Dinamo Zagreb, making Dinamo clear favourites at 58.4% against NEC's 19.8% and noting that Tadić was missing. The absence is reported again [3], and NEC still arrive on the same winless run we described then.

The price holds both truths

NEC are the better team on the ball and the worse team at turning it into points. Groningen are the better-placed team with the more open defence. The market holds both facts at once: a home favourite at 41.7% but only a quarter of a goal better, and a goal line pushed above three. Sunday is likely to turn on whether NEC's chance creation finally lands. The price makes them the most likely winner; it does not make them a likely one.
