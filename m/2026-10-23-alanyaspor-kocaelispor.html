A response needed, a defence missing

Alanyaspor went to Fenerbahçe on Saturday and came home with a 0-3 defeat, Romelu Lukaku scoring his first Süper Lig goal in that game according to beIN SPORTS' match summary [8]. Six days later they are back in Alanya, and the table says plenty about the size of this fixture. Alanyaspor are 9th on 12 points; Kocaelispor are 8th on exactly the same number.

The home side are still clear favourites. With the margin removed, Alanyaspor are 47.5% to win, the draw is 29.8% and Kocaelispor are 22.6%. That gap is not built on league position, because there is none. It is built on home advantage and on a visiting defence that arrives short-handed.

Kocaelispor, though, are not arriving quietly. They have lost their last two, both by a single goal, and their coach has spent the week answering the same question about the health of his squad.

Points without control

Alanyaspor's run before the Fenerbahçe trip was steady without being dominant: two wins and two draws, including a 0-0 at home to Erzurumspor and a 2-2 with Göztepe. Even in those unbeaten games the chance numbers were not on their side. Over the last five they have created 1.38 expected goals a game and allowed 1.94, so the points have come with a negative balance.

Kocaelispor's results read worse than their performances. They lost 1-2 at home to Göztepe on 18 October while out-creating them 1.61 to 0.83, and they went down narrowly at Beşiktaş a week earlier. Both teams have scored five goals in their last five matches, which fits a tight game more than an open one. Kocaelispor have had five days to recover, Alanyaspor six.

Two holes in the away back line

Kocaelispor's problem is where it hurts most. Defender M. Haidara is out with a muscle injury after six appearances, 474 minutes, one goal and a 7.15 rating; fellow defender T. Zoukrou is also out with a muscle injury after five appearances and 416 minutes. A. Jovanovic is missing by the coach's decision and B. Petkovic has a hamstring injury. Selçuk İnan said injuries have become a cliché for his side this season, according to sondakika.com [3].

İnan also said he expects Alanyaspor to set up with a three-man defence, and that Matej Maglica could start if he is not judged a risk, according to haberler.com [1][2]. Maglica himself said the team wants to return from Alanya with a win, according to haberler.com [5], and the squad is preparing under the message "Parola 3 Puan" — password three points — according to Özgün Kocaeli [6]. For Alanyaspor, a Shango Media match preview reports P. Fernandes as doubtful with an injury [9]; he has 215 minutes this season and one goal.

Half a goal better, no more

The handicap line is Alanyaspor -0.5, which means the home side has to win the match for that side of the line to land; a draw is enough for Kocaelispor +0.5. The fair line sits at -0.44, so the market rates Alanyaspor roughly half a goal the better team — the value of playing at home, not a mismatch. That is consistent with a side that has conceded more chance quality than it has created.

The goals line is 2.25, with over at 46.5% and both teams to score at 46.7%: the market leans towards a single-goal game. This week the home win chance eased from 50.0% to 47.5%, the draw rose 1.7 points and over 2.25 fell 1.7 points, while the fair handicap moved from -0.51 to -0.44. Those moves followed the defeat at Fenerbahçe and are consistent with the market trimming the favourite, not abandoning him.

Two reasons, one worry

Alanyaspor are favourites for two connected reasons: they are at home, and Kocaelispor arrive without two of their most-used defenders. The 0-3 at Fenerbahçe explains the small drift in the price but not a change of favourite — it was their only league defeat in five, at a stronger side, and it followed two wins and two draws.

The worry for the home team is the chance balance. Allowing 1.94 expected goals a game while creating 1.38 says they give up real opportunities, and that is why the handicap is only about half a goal and the goals line sits low. Kocaelispor are creating enough to score, as the Göztepe game showed, but they keep losing the fine margins — and with a patched defence, those margins get harder to win.
