Units over time

How we count: one unit on every pick. A win at a price of 2.00 is +1 unit, a win at 3.00 is +2 units, a loss is −1 unit. Handicap and goal lines can return half or the whole stake. Prices are the market's average when the pick was published. Market move = how many points the market's chance for the pick rose (+) or fell (−) between publication and kick-off. The verdict reads both: Right won and the market moved our way, Lucky won but it moved away, Unlucky lost but it moved our way, Wrong lost and it moved away.
